**Onchain should be necessary, not decorative**

"Built onchain" or "decentralized" is not enough. The product story must explain what breaks without onchain infrastructure.

**The solution should use Arbitrum/Robinhood Chain or onchain infrastructure** because the problem needs at least one of:

- Verifiability
- Programmability
- Composability
- Asset ownership
- Transparency

Usually these properties boil down to solutions that either **1) reduce costs** (e.g. by reducing counterparty risk, removing manual workflows, etc), or **2) significantly improve users life**.

**Embrace your team**

Your team is full of people who are experts in different domains and can help define your product and wedges.

**Work across your organization:**

- **Business Development** team has the most insight into customers experience and new opportunity spaces, these should heavily inform Product
- **Engineering and Research** should be involved early in product solution ideation – define requirements and attributes you want to achieve, don't pose them solutions (example: Parallel Execution != a attribute)
- **DevRel** has hands on experience working with your tools and hear customer feedback, they understand the pain points of your product
- **Marketing** sees the overall market landscape and positioning, they should be involved early in building
- **User-journey & UX** should be thought of from the beginning, not just after designing a solution

Don't be lazy or expect that information to naturally surface, establish funnels that cater to positive reinforcement loops.

**Product workflow example**

1. **Hypotheses**: Build hypothesis, opportunities, and specific customer personas
2. **Validate**: Talk to customers and get pain points to validate/invalidate hypotheses
3. **Solutions & Alternatives**: Look for solutions that address opportunities. Examine alternatives and competitive solutions
4. **Plan**: Pick highest-return components (not necessarily full solution) you can ship within X months
5. **Execute & Reiterate**: Execute plan, and reiterate through this workfow throughout the build.
